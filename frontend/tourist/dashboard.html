Trekking & Hiking

Monsoon Rajgad Fort Citadel Trek

📍 Pune / Raigad, Maharashtra

High-ridge ascent through Padmavati Machi and Suvela Machi with panoramic Western Ghats vistas and Maratha citadel bastions.

⏱️ Duration: 5 - 6 Hours
💪 Difficulty: Moderate-Difficult
☀️ Best Season: Jul – Feb
🧭 Accompaniment: Certified Guide
