Heritage Protection & Legal Regulations

This destination is a nationally protected monument and eco-sensitive heritage zone. Visitors must comply with statutory preservation laws:

  • Monument Integrity: Scratching, defacing, carving, or removing masonry stones from fort walls or rock-cut caves is a non-bailable offence under the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act.
  • Zero-Plastic Policy: Single-use plastic bottles, snack packets, and polythene carry bags are strictly barred at base camp security gates. Visitors must carry reusable water canteens.
  • Camping & Open Fires: Lighting bonfires, cooking with open flames, or overnight wilderness camping outside registered base camp campsites is strictly prohibited.
  • Drone Flights: Operating unmanned aerial vehicles (drones) requires prior written clearance from the District Administration and ASI circle offices.

Seasonal Climate & Visiting Conditions

Peak Season Window

October – February

Clear skies, crisp mountain air, and safe dry rock faces. Daylight exploration recommended between 06:30 AM and 05:30 PM.

Monsoon Window

July – September

Lush greenery and cascading waterfalls. Slippery rock patches require ankle-support hiking boots and accompaniment by registered local guides.
