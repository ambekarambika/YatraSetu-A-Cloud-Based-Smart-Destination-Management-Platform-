🛡️ YatraSetu Architectural Governance Principles

1. Locked Destination Integrity Once a stakeholder registration is approved by the Destination Manager, the stakeholder cannot alter their operating destination via ordinary profile editing.
2. Controlled Reassignment & Audit Trail Authorized corrections or reassignments require an official written justification and generate an administrative audit log with actor attribution.
3. Exclusive Account Governance Only the Platform Administrator holds the authority to activate, deactivate, or delete user and stakeholder accounts.
4. Four Official Roles "State Manager" is an elevated access level within Destination Manager (scope: 'STATE'), not a separate fifth role.

🏛️ State-Level Access (State Manager)

Elevated permission scope within Destination Manager (scope: 'STATE'). Visibility extends across all destinations within the assigned state.

⚠️ State visibility does not authorize reviews outside assigned state, and does NOT grant account activation, deactivation, or deletion privileges.
📍 Destination-Level Access

Standard scope (scope: 'DESTINATION'). Operational and business registration approvals remain strictly confined to the single assigned destination jurisdiction.

🛡️ Cannot review or decide business registrations outside the assigned destination territory.
⚖️ Controlled Reassignment

Only Platform Administrators can alter manager territory or promote/demote scope. Requires mandatory written administrative justification.

📜 Every reassignment creates an audit trail entry with actor attribution, previous/new jurisdiction, and reason.
